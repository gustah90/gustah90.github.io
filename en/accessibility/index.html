Accessibility and verification

This project aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Conformance has not yet been demonstrated.

The foundation includes semantic HTML, a skip link, visible focus, explicit language selection, and reduced motion support.

Before claiming conformance, all applicable criteria must be assessed, including keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, and content reflow.

Automated checks cover only part of the requirements and do not replace manual evaluation.
